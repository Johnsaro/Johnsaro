How scoring works

Each project is reviewed and earns a skill gain for its language.

PartWhat counts
FundamentalsMatters most. Using a concept for the first time earns the most.
LengthAdds 2 pts at most — more lines alone don't help much.
QualityMultiplier from ×0.5 (messy) to ×1.2 (clean).

Limits that keep it honest:

  • Max +8% per project.
  • Diminishing returns — gains shrink as the % gets higher.
  • Gates at 40%, 70% and 90% — to pass one, a project has to use harder concepts.
